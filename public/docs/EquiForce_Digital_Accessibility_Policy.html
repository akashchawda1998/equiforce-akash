1. Purpose

This Policy establishes how EquiForce Solutions Inc. ("EquiForce" or the "Company") designs, builds, procures, publishes, and maintains digital products, services, and content so that they are usable by people with disabilities.

It exists to do four things:

  • Set a single, testable technical standard so that "accessible" means the same thing to every team;
  • Assign named accountability, so that accessibility is owned rather than assumed;
  • Define how the Company responds when someone tells us they cannot use something we built;
  • Create a durable, dated record of a good-faith accessibility program.

1.1 Policy statement

EquiForce is committed to providing digital products, services, and content that people with disabilities can use independently and effectively. The Company adopts the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as its technical standard, applies that standard across all digital properties, and treats accessibility as a requirement of release rather than a post-release correction.

1.2 Legal context

EquiForce is a private company and a place of public accommodation for purposes of Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act. WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the standard U.S. courts, the Department of Justice, and settlement agreements consistently apply.

  • California (Unruh Act): California's Unruh Civil Rights Act carries minimum statutory damages of $4,000 per violation. EquiForce is California-domiciled.
  • Client flow-down: Public pension plans, state retirement systems, and public university endowments are subject to ADA Title II; their obligations arrive at EquiForce through procurement contracts.
  • Employment (ADA Title I): Obligations attach to job postings, online applications, assessment tools, and internal systems once the Company reaches 15 employees.

2. Definitions

Term Meaning
Accessibility Standard WCAG 2.1 Level AA, published by the W3C, together with any successor version adopted under Section 4.2.
Accessibility Defect (a) Any failure to satisfy the Accessibility Standard; or (b) any characteristic that prevents or materially impairs use by a person with a disability using assistive technology.
Core User Journey Any sequence of interactions by which a person may learn about EquiForce services, submit a form, create or access an account, retrieve or read a report, or apply for employment.
Alternate Access An equally effective means of obtaining the same information or completing the same task, provided at no additional cost and without undue delay.
Overlay A client-side widget or plug-in that modifies a rendered page after delivery. Prohibited as a primary accessibility measure under Section 4.3.
Release Gate The mandatory set of automated and manual accessibility checks required before deploying code to production.

3. Scope

This Policy applies to all digital properties developed internally, by contractors, or licensed from third parties:

  • Public website at equiforce.ai, landing pages, campaign pages, and gated content
  • EquiForce Platform UI and authenticated client portal
  • Reports, dashboards, and documents generated by the Platform (performance measurement, GIPS composite reporting, AI investment reporting)
  • Sales collateral, whitepapers, case studies, and digital decks
  • Careers pages, job postings, application forms, and applicant tracking systems
  • Video and audio content, webinars, and explainer media
  • Email templates and marketing campaigns
  • Internal systems used by employees to perform job duties
  • Third-party embedded components (chat, consent management, forms, scheduling, analytics)

4. The Standard & Overlay Prohibition

4.1 Standard adopted

EquiForce adopts WCAG 2.1 Level AA as its standard across all 50 success criteria. New builds target WCAG 2.2 Level AA where feasible.

4.3 Overlays prohibited

Overlays Prohibited: EquiForce will not use an Overlay product to satisfy, or represent that it satisfies, any requirement of this Policy. Conformance must be achieved in underlying code, markup, and design.

8. Feedback and Complaint Response SLA

  • Intake: Any employee receiving feedback must forward to accessibility@equiforce.ai within 1 business day.
  • Acknowledgement: Acknowledged within five (5) business days.
  • Alternate Access: Provided within three (3) business days if a barrier prevents task completion.
  • Mandatory Escalation: Immediate escalation to Legal/Executive Sponsor if attorney, demand letter, or statute is cited.

9. Defect Severity & Remediation Targets

Severity Definition Target
Critical Prevents a user from completing a Core User Journey (keyboard trap, unlabeled form, broken nav). 10 business days (Alternate Access in 3 days)
High Materially impairs completion of a Core User Journey. 30 calendar days
Medium Level AA failure outside a Core User Journey. 60 calendar days
Low Minimal practical impact on assistive technology users. Next release / 90 days
