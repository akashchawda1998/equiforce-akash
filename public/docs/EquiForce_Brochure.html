Core Solutions & Platform Capabilities

1 Performance Measurement & Attribution

Comprehensive portfolio returns engine engineered for complex multi-asset strategies.

  • Daily Time-Weighted (TWR) and Money-Weighted (MWR / IRR) returns
  • Brinson-Fachler, Brinson-Hood-Beebower & multi-currency attribution
  • Comprehensive benchmark tracking, tracking error, beta, and Sharpe ratios
  • Automated cash flow timing and valuation reconciliation

2 GIPS® Composite Management

End-to-end composite lifecycle management compliant with CFA Institute GIPS® standards.

  • Automated account inclusion/exclusion rules and mandate classification
  • Asset-weighted and equal-weighted composite dispersion calculations
  • Ex-post 3-year annualized standard deviation metrics
  • Audit-ready presentation reports ready for third-party verifiers

3 AI-Powered Reporting & Intelligence

Next-generation generative reporting that turns complex data into executive clarity.

  • Automated natural-language portfolio commentary and performance briefs
  • Real-time anomaly detection and outlier flagging across holdings
  • Dynamic dashboard exports (PDF, Excel, interactive client portals)
  • Instant synthesis of quarterly review packs for investment committees

Hands-on partnership with seasoned industry practitioners to scale operations.

  • Operating model assessment and workflow automation roadmaps
  • Custodian and accounting system integration (Custodians, PAM, Advent, etc.)
  • Historical data migration and track record recalculation
  • Interim operations leadership and staff augmentation
